Our Story

17 Years of Culinary Excellence

Videsi began with a journey through hotel management and the world of five-star hospitality, mastering diverse Indian and international cuisines and the fine details of flavour, quality and presentation.

Today that experience lives in downtown Kitchener. At Videsi, every dish is thoughtfully crafted and every table becomes part of the journey: the comfort of home-style Punjabi cooking, the fun of Desi Chinese and the generosity of a true Indian thaal.

Akash PatelHead Chef
Chef’s Journey

From Five-Star Kitchens to King Street

◆

Before Videsi, Chef Akash Patel spent years in India’s leading five-star hotels, cooking for and welcoming film stars, singers, cricketers and distinguished public figures. That same care now goes into every plate in downtown Kitchener.
