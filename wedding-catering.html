Good to Know

Wedding Catering FAQ

◆
How many guests can you cater for at a wedding?

Our large production kitchen caters weddings and receptions from 100 to 2,000+ guests across Kitchener, Waterloo and Cambridge.

Which wedding events do you cater?

Mehndi, haldi, sangeet, garba and raas nights, the wedding day, receptions and engagement parties. You can book us for one event or the whole wedding.

Can we customize the wedding menu?

Yes. We plan the menu with you from our full range of Punjabi curries, Matka Biryani, paneer and tandoori dishes, fresh naan, chaat, Desi Chinese and Indian sweets, with vegetarian options for every guest.

Can we do a menu tasting?

Contact us to discuss your wedding menu and tasting options when you request your quote.

How early should we book wedding catering?

Book as early as possible, especially for large weddings and peak wedding season, so we can reserve your date and plan your menu.
